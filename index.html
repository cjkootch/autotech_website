FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to pay anything to see a mockup?

No. I'll put together a website mockup or a phone receptionist demo for your business first. You decide after you've seen it.

How long does setup take?

About a week. I handle the setup and testing; you answer a few questions about your services, hours and service area.

Is there a contract?

Everything is month-to-month. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. The only exception is the $0-down website option ($99/mo), which has a 12-month minimum.

What's included in the monthly price?

Hosting, upkeep, and normal call and text volume. Very high call or text volume is quoted up front so there are no surprises.

Will the AI receptionist sound like a robot to my customers?

It answers in a natural voice, handles common questions, books estimates, and texts you a summary of every call. You can hear a demo set up for your business before you pay anything.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Yes. You can add missed-call text-back, the AI receptionist, review requests or a Google Business Profile tune-up on their own, without a new website.

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Tomball, TX and work with service businesses across Tomball and north Houston. The setup itself is done remotely, so I can help businesses outside the area too.
